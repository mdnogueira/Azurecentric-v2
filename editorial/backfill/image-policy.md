# Article images

Images are optional. Use a Microsoft Learn/Docs screenshot or illustration only when it directly explains the article's subject and the adjacent procedure. Check the actual pixels, product, task and documentation revision. Historical articles need evidence available by their editorial date.

Do not insert a standard checkpoint diagram, decorative stock image, or mandatory lead illustration into every article. A useful bespoke explanatory diagram can remain, but it must explain actual relationships rather than repeat a numbered list in boxes.

The listing thumbnail is the first image in the article body. When the body has no image, the listing uses `/images/logo-symbol.png`. Frontmatter must not introduce a different thumbnail rule.

Keep screenshot source URLs, revisions, hashes and review notes in editorial records. Do not append visible Microsoft Learn product screenshot or permission captions. Retain meaningful image alt text.
